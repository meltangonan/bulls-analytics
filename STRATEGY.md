# Editorial direction lives in Notion

[Editorial direction](https://www.notion.so/3d2e1c13abe681e586b4c44762261fab) owns audience, editorial
choices, voice, captions, distribution, and review principles. Update that page when those decisions
change; do not maintain a second strategy here.

The [posts database](https://www.notion.so/3a6e1c13abe6809ab16bfe33edb233cf) owns individual briefs,
status, provenance, Canva links, publication links, and results.

Read the relevant Notion context when choosing or rescoping work. A routine implementation adjustment
can proceed from the settled brief already in the task. If Notion is unavailable, use confirmed
conversation context and state any unresolved editorial dependency rather than inventing live state.
