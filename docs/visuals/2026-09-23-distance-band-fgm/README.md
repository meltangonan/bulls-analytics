# Bulls player-season field-goal leaders by distance

Current Canva handoff: 16 text-free, 2700 x 2538 court assets at 300 DPI, one winning player-season per distance group. Each image is cropped to the sidelines, baseline, and half-court line, with no blank page around the court. All court markings, including the sidelines and borders, are black; the other distance bands are gray and the active band is Bulls red. The final asset highlights shots at 30 feet or farther within the offensive half-court. The midcourt boundary includes the basket-facing halves of the official 6-ft-radius center circle and 2-ft-radius inner circle. Add headshots, player details, and distance labels separately in Canva. `data/carousel-values.csv` is the exact 16-row handoff table. FGM is the ranking metric and PPS breaks FGM ties. Earlier versions with blank composition space or portrait details are review history.

## Scope and calculation

- Chicago Bulls regular seasons from 1997-98 through 2025-26. A player traded during a season contributes only shots taken for Chicago.
- One NBA `ShotChartDetail` row is one field-goal attempt. `SHOT_DISTANCE` is feet from the basket, rounded down to a whole foot from `LOC_X` and `LOC_Y`; the coordinates are in tenths of a foot.
- Bands are half-open: 0-2 means 0 <= distance < 2; 2-4 means 2 <= distance < 4, and so on through 28-30. The final group includes shots with `SHOT_DISTANCE >= 30` and `LOC_Y < 417.5` (in tenths of a foot), the straight half-court line relative to the basket. A shot near a sideline can have radial distance above 41 ft 9 in while remaining within the offensive half-court. Backcourt attempts are excluded.
- Rank player-seasons within each band by made field goals (`SHOT_MADE_FLAG` summed). Break an FGM tie with higher PPS; if PPS also ties exactly, use fewer FGA, then player ID and season for a deterministic single winner. The 28-30 ft FGM tie goes to Coby White 2023-24 (21 FGM, 1.189 PPS) over Zach LaVine 2022-23 (21 FGM, 1.167 PPS).
- `PPS = (2 * made twos + 3 * made threes) / field-goal attempts` for the winning player-season in that same band. Two- versus three-point value comes from NBA `SHOT_TYPE`, not distance. Free throws are outside this field-goal statistic.

The band around the arc can contain both twos and threes. For example, Ben Gordon's 2005-06 22-24 ft row contains 22 made twos and 42 made threes: 64 FGM on 135 FGA, so PPS is `(22*2 + 42*3) / 135 = 1.259259...`. The leaderboard ranks his 64 makes; PPS describes the value of his attempts in that band.

## Source and audit

- NBA `ShotChartDetail`: `https://stats.nba.com/stats/shotchartdetail`, via `nba_api.stats.endpoints.shotchartdetail.ShotChartDetail`, with `team_id=1610612741`, `player_id=0`, each season, `season_type_all_star="Regular Season"`, `context_measure_simple="FGA"`. Saved raw responses: `data/shotchartdetail/<season>.csv.gz`; capture times and hashes: `data/source-manifest.csv`.
- NBA `LeagueDashTeamStats`: `https://stats.nba.com/stats/leaguedashteamstats`, via `nba_api.stats.endpoints.leaguedashteamstats.LeagueDashTeamStats`, with each season, `season_type_all_star="Regular Season"`, `per_mode_detailed="Totals"`, then the Chicago Bulls team row. Saved official FGM/FGA/3PM/3PA: `data/official-team-totals.csv`.
- All 29 shot-log seasons match the official team FGM, FGA, 3PM and 3PA totals: `data/source-reconciliation.csv`. Across those seasons, ten field-goal attempts have no location, including two made threes; they cannot enter a distance band. There are 1,004 attempts and 103 makes at 30+ feet overall. Of these, 694 attempts and 91 makes are in the offensive half-court; 310 attempts and 12 makes are beyond its midcourt boundary. See `data/30-to-halfcourt-audit.csv` and `data/location-exclusions.csv`.
- `data/all-player-seasons.csv` retains every 0-30 ft player-season/band aggregate; `data/distance-band-leaders.csv` retains the selected winner per band; `data/distance-band-fgm-ties.csv` retains all FGM co-leaders before the PPS tiebreak. `data/30-to-halfcourt-player-seasons.csv` and `data/30-to-halfcourt-leader.csv` cover the final group. `data/carousel-values.csv` combines all 16 selected winners. The earlier unbounded `30-plus-*` files remain as sensitivity history. All are produced by `scripts/prototypes/distance_band_fgm.py`.
- Sensitivity from the saved shot rows: among 1,004 attempts at 30+ ft, 59 makes were at 30–31 ft, 20 at 32–33 ft, and 24 at 34+ ft. The farthest recorded make was 52 ft; the farthest attempt was 87 ft. At 34+ ft, no Bulls player-season made more than two shots. A single 30+ group preserves a meaningful winner without promoting one-make two-foot groups to equal slides.
- `scripts/prototypes/distance_band_fgm_court_assets.py --final` renders the 16 current court-only assets. Its court markers use `bulls.graphics.court.draw_chart_court`, the shared black-ink and selected-lane-mark convention now used by other Bulls court chart families; the band plates come from the established ladder helper. Current files are `assets/2026-09-24-v22-distance-band-*-court.png`, packaged in `assets/2026-09-24-v22-distance-band-court-only-final.zip`; generated working files are in `output/distance-band-fgm/court-only-final/`. The half-court line is drawn at 41 ft 9 in from the basket (47 ft from the baseline minus the basket's 5 ft 3 in offset). The center-circle radii follow the [official 2025–26 NBA court diagram](https://cdn.nba.com/manage/2026/01/Official-2025-26-NBA-Playing-Rules.pdf). V20 remains as the reviewed cropped version with dark-gray court markings; v21 changes the markings to black at 900 x 846; v22 is the 300-DPI export of that composition. The earlier `distance_band_fgm_chart.py` rendered three v16 portrait-and-label examples. Source portraits and attribution remain in `data/portraits/`. The regulation court helper retains its full marking set for official-diagram use. The v21 court assets remain as reviewed history.

These are absolute season totals, not rates adjusted for games played or season length. 1997-98 is the first season after the NBA restored its standard three-point line. The arc is 23 ft 9 in above the break and 22 ft in the corners; the distance-band classification is independent of that line.
