# Court-family review renders

These 300-DPI PNGs show how the shared black court markings appear across the existing chart families. They are review examples, not replacements for previously published posts. The shot-based examples use saved 2025-26 Matas Buzelis shot data; the roster and scoring-by-location examples use their corresponding 2025-26 data. The summer-league file is a court-only geometry check without shot data.

The common markings are the sidelines, baseline, half-court line and center-circle halves where the full offensive half-court is shown; the basket, arc and paint; and lane-edge ticks at 7, 8, 11 and 14 feet from the baseline. The ticks at 7 and 8 feet form the double mark. All court lines are black. Individual chart families retain their established orientation, crop and data display.

The 11 full-size review charts are in `assets/2026-09-24-v01-*.png` and together in `assets/2026-09-24-v01-court-family-review-final.zip`. The contact sheet is `assets/2026-09-24-v01-contact-sheet.png`. The distance-band Canva courts are separate, in `../2026-09-23-distance-band-fgm/assets/2026-09-24-v22-distance-band-court-only-final.zip`.
